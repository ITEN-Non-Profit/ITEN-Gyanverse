* **Book Link:** [Help Desk and Service Desk Pocket Interview Guide](https://github.com/ITEN-Non-Profit/ITEN-Gyanverse/blob/main/professional-it-career/system-administration/interview-preparation/markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md) *(Local file: [`./markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md`](./markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md))*

* **Author:** Jiwan Bhattarai · IT Education Nepal (2026)
* **Target Audience:** University students, L1 and entry-level IT support candidates, and early L2 candidates reviewing core fundamentals.

### Short Description

The [Help Desk and Service Desk Pocket Interview Guide](https://github.com/ITEN-Non-Profit/ITEN-Gyanverse/blob/main/professional-it-career/system-administration/interview-preparation/markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md) is a structured interview preparation manual covering **86 technical and behavioral questions (`Q1` to `Q86`)** across five progressive stages. Every question is formatted with four practical components:
* **Answer:** A clear, structured response ready for technical interviews.
* **Follow-up:** Likely probing questions interviewers ask next.
* **Scenario:** A real-world support ticket or caller situation.
* **Mentor's Advice:** Practical guidance on how to explain your reasoning clearly.

In addition to the 86 questions, the handbook includes rapid-fire revision checklists, essential diagnostic commands, mock interview evaluation sheets, a 7-day revision plan, a priority grid with communication templates, and a technical glossary.
