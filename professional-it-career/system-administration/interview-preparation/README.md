## 1. Help Desk and Service Desk Pocket Interview Guide

**Practice the technical skills and communication expected in an IT support interview.**

This guide is for university students, entry-level applicants, L1 support candidates, and early L2 technicians reviewing core fundamentals. Its five stages connect everyday support work with interview preparation: understand the issue, investigate it, explain the next step, and confirm the outcome.

**[Read the Help Desk and Service Desk Pocket Interview Guide →](./markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md)**

[Browse the contents](./markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md#contents) · [Try a mock interview](./markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md#56-mock-interview-and-job-description) · [Follow the seven-day revision plan](./markdown/Help_Desk_Service_Desk_Pocket_Interview_Guide-Final.md#57-seven-day-revision-plan)

**Author:** Jiwan Bhattarai · **Publisher:** IT Education Nepal (ITEN) · **Edition:** 2026  
**Inside:** 86 technical and behavioral interview questions across five stages, with follow-ups, scenarios, and revision tools.

### How to practice with each question

- **Answer:** Learn the main points, then explain them in your own words.
- **Follow-up:** Test whether you can explain the reasoning behind your answer.
- **Scenario:** Apply the topic to a support ticket or caller situation.
- **Mentor’s Advice:** Review how to communicate clearly, work within your role, and recognize when to escalate.

### Explore the five stages

| Stage | Questions | Main topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Support roles and communication** | Q1–Q20 | Role responsibilities; ITSM, SLAs, and ticket lifecycles; customer communication and de-escalation. |
| **2. Troubleshooting and endpoint support** | Q21–Q38 | Troubleshooting methods, Windows support, printers and peripherals, macOS, and managed devices. |
| **3. Identity, access, and networking** | Q39–Q58 | Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, MFA, RBAC, TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, IPv4/IPv6, diagnostic commands, and VPN support. |
| **4. Microsoft 365 and security** | Q59–Q74 | Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint permissions, endpoint security, phishing, safe support practices, and knowledge base documentation. |
| **5. Interview performance and revision** | Q75–Q86 + revision tools | Introducing yourself, showing evidence, handling mistakes and uncertainty, escalation, and closing the interview. |

The guide also includes rapid-fire checklists, essential diagnostic commands, mock interview evaluation sheets, a seven-day revision plan, a priority grid, communication templates, and a technical glossary.

**Try this:** Answer one question aloud before reading the model answer. Then work through its follow-up and scenario. Use the revision tools to track the topics you need to practice again.

## 2. The SysAdmin Interview Playbook

**Practice the reasoning behind system administration answers—from the first symptom to a verified result.**

Use this book when you are preparing for IT support or junior system administrator interviews, or moving from help desk work into administration. It focuses on Active Directory and Windows Server, core networking, Microsoft Intune, and Microsoft Entra ID with Microsoft 365. More experienced candidates can use the scenario drills for review.

**[Read The SysAdmin Interview Playbook →](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md)**

[Browse the contents](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#contents) · [Practice scenarios](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-5) · [Review before an interview](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-6)

**Author:** Jiwan Bhattarai · **Publisher:** IT Education Nepal (ITEN) · **Edition:** 2026  
**Inside:** 54 interview questions, 54 follow-up questions, eight scenario drills, quick recaps, a glossary, and 87 linked sources.

### How the answers work

- **Short answer:** Give a direct answer to the interviewer’s question.
- **In practice:** Connect the concept to a task, example, tool, or test result.
- **Follow-up and Suggested response:** Test your understanding, then compare your reasoning with the model response.

Questions carry **Entry**, **Junior**, or **Administrator** level tags, sometimes in combination. Practical notes, command examples, and mentor guidance help you explain what you would check, why you would check it, and how you would confirm the result.

### Explore the six sections

| Section | What is included | Main topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[1. Active Directory and Windows Server](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-1)** | 14 questions + recap | AD DS, domain controllers, DNS, DHCP, Group Policy, permissions, FSMO roles, replication, PowerShell, recovery, and hardening. |
| **[2. Core networking](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-2)** | 15 questions + recap | IP and MAC addresses, network models, subnetting, NAT, DNS, gateways, VLANs, VPNs, TCP/UDP, ports, Wi-Fi, diagnostic commands, and IPv6. |
| **[3. Microsoft Intune](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-3)** | 13 questions + recap | MDM and MAM, enrollment, compliance, Windows Autopilot, device identity, app deployment, detection rules, app protection, and troubleshooting. |
| **[4. Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft 365](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-4)** | 12 questions + recap | Hybrid identity, password hash synchronization, MFA, Conditional Access, SSO, mailboxes and groups, sign-in logs, guest access, and licensing. |
| **[5. Scenario drills](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-5)** | Eight practical scenarios | Branch logon failures, Group Policy, DHCP, device compliance, DNS, suspected account compromise, Windows services, and mail delivery. |
| **[6. Rapid revision](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#section-6)** | Checklist + six-question self-test | Final preparation, useful log and report locations, and an answer key for checking your reasoning. |

Question numbers restart within each of the first four sections. Use the section question lists to find a topic, the return links to move around the book, and the [linked references](./markdown/The_SysAdmin_Interview_Playbook_2026.md#references) to check the supporting documentation.

**Try this:** Choose one question, explain the short answer aloud, and work through its follow-up. Then use a scenario to practice scope, evidence, action, verification, and prevention. If you have not performed a task, say so and describe how you would practice it in an authorized lab or with a senior engineer.

## Build a practice routine

1. **Choose a topic from the job description.** Read the matching section and note unfamiliar terms.
2. **Answer aloud before checking the model.** Explain the idea clearly, then add a relevant example.
3. **Work through the follow-up or scenario.** Say what you would check, what the result would tell you, and how you would verify a fix.
4. **Review the gaps.** Keep a short practice list and repeat the questions you could not explain confidently.

Use authorized labs or test environments for practical work. Be clear about what you have done yourself and what you would do with guidance. These books support interview preparation; production work must follow your organization’s procedures and permissions.
