# AI Career Transformation Hub for IT Interview and CV Preparation

Free IT interview preparation, CV and resume templates, and AI practice prompts from IT Education Nepal. Use the English-language notes alongside the Nepali workshop recordings to prepare for entry-level IT support, help desk, networking, cloud, and other technical roles in Nepal or abroad.

## Mission

**Mission 10,000 Skilled Engineers** helps learners build practical IT skills and prepare for work in Nepal and abroad.

## What This Repository Covers

1. [7-Day CV and Resume Mission](01-cv-and-resume-mission/README.md). Build a CV, cover letter, and LinkedIn profile using the [CV templates and job application prompts](cv-and-job-application-notes/README.md).
2. [IT Interview Preparation With AI](02-interview-preparation-with-ai/README.md). Use the [mock interview prompts and STAR examples](02-interview-preparation-with-ai/01-ai-interview-prep-session-guide.md) or practice with the [IT support and help desk interview questions](02-interview-preparation-with-ai/02-it-support-question-bank.md).
3. [Day-to-Day Troubleshooting With AI](#03-day-to-day-troubleshooting-guide-with-ai) (planned module)

## 01. 7-Day CV Mission

Build a CV, cover letter, and LinkedIn profile using AI and mentor feedback. This series is for students, new graduates, self-taught learners, career changers, and job seekers in Nepal and abroad.

### What You Will Learn

- How to create your first CV from scratch
- How to optimize your CV for real job applications
- How to use AI tools for CV and resume improvement
- How to write a professional career summary
- How to present skills, projects, education, and experience
- How to write a cover letter using AI
- How to build and optimize a LinkedIn profile
- How to avoid common CV, resume, cover letter, and LinkedIn mistakes
- How to prepare your profile for Nepal and global job markets

- How to turn job descriptions into realistic practice questions
- How to convert your CV into strong interview answers
- How to explain your projects, labs, and experience confidently
- How to use AI for mock interviews
- How to build your personal Interview Evidence Bank
- How to use STAR, SOAR, CAR, and PREP frameworks
- How to answer scenario questions and explain troubleshooting
- How to prepare for Nepal, GCC, Europe, North America, and remote global IT job markets

## Recommended AI Tools

Use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for drafting and mock practice. For public company or industry research, use a tool that provides source links, such as Perplexity, and check the original sources. Use your own evidence and verify AI feedback before applying it.

3. Move to **Interview Preparation With AI**
4. Practice HR, technical, and mock interviews
5. Build your Interview Evidence Bank
6. Use the troubleshooting resources in the third module
7. Learn practical IT diagnostics and real problem solving
8. Keep improving your GitHub, LinkedIn, CV, and technical projects
9. Apply for internships, jobs, remote roles, and global opportunities
