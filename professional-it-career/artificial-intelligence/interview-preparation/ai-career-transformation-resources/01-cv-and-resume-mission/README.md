# 7-Day CV and Resume Mission

Build a first IT resume, tailor it to a job description, and prepare a cover letter and LinkedIn profile. This guide links to the CV workshop recordings and six practice notes.

## What is the 7-Day CV Mission?

IT Education Nepal organized a free, seven-day initiative for Nepali learners with a target of reviewing 150 CVs. Mentor Jiwan Bhattarai reviewed student CVs during live sessions and demonstrated how to revise them using AI and individual feedback.

## Who the mission is for

- Final-year students and fresh graduates preparing for their first IT roles.
- Students who have had limited access to career guidance.
- Self-taught learners and career changers who want to document their skills.
- Beginners writing their first CV.

## Mentor

Jiwan Bhattarai, CCIE #60261 and CEH, teaches and mentors through IT Education Nepal. His work includes enterprise networking, security, data centers, cloud, and IT operations.
