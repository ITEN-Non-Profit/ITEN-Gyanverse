## Core Frameworks Covered

### STAR Framework

**Situation, Task, Action, Result**

Use for behavioral interview questions.

- Tell me about a challenge you faced
- Tell me about a time you solved a problem
- Tell me about a time you worked in a team

### SOAR Framework

**Situation, Obstacle, Action, Result**

Use for career stories that show how you handled a setback.

### CAR Framework

**Challenge or Context, Action, Result**

Use for brief problem-solving answers and troubleshooting stories.

### PREP Framework

**Point, Reason, Example, Point**

Use for opinions and questions about your leadership approach.

## 30-Day Practice Routine

### Daily Practice, 45 Minutes

| Time | Activity |
| ---: | --- |
| 10 min | Review one job description. |
| 10 min | Practice one HR question. |
| 15 min | Practice one technical or scenario question. |
| 5 min | Ask AI to review your answer. |
| 5 min | Rewrite the answer in your own voice. |

### Weekly Practice

- Complete one full mock interview
- Improve your CV and LinkedIn based on interview gaps
- Update your GitHub or project portfolio
- Record yourself answering questions
- Score clarity, structure, and technical accuracy with the [mock interview rubric](01-ai-interview-prep-session-guide.md#101-scoring-rubric). Repeat one question to check improvement.

## Recommended AI Tools

Use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for text-based mock interviews and answer feedback. Use Perplexity or another tool that provides source links for public company research. Check the sources and verify technical feedback against official documentation. Base every answer on projects, labs, and experience you can explain.
