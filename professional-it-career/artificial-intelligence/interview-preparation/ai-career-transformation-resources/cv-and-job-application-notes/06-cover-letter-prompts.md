# AI Cover Letter Prompts for IT Job Applications

IT Education Nepal · © 2026

Draft an IT cover letter from your CV and a job description. Use the opening, body, and closing prompts with verified examples from your own work.

```text
1 Introduction / Opening
2-3 Body Paragraphs
Closing Statement
```

A strong cover letter targets a **specific role or company**, showcasing your relevant experience through brief stories that demonstrate your skills. To do this effectively, research the job description and the company's website or LinkedIn to understand their valued traits and skills.

## Complete Cover Letter Prompt

Fill in the bracketed inputs. Use verified details from the CV, the job posting, and the employer's public materials.

### Full Cover Letter, Prompt A

```text
Act as a cover letter editor for IT roles. Using my CV and the job description, write a
customized one-page cover letter for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY NAME].

CV: [Paste relevant CV details with private information removed]

JD: [ Paste JD ]

Connection to the company: [Mention why you're interested in this company]

- Match my CV evidence to the job requirements. Mark missing details for me to verify.
- Strong self-introduction with an engaging hook
- Highlight relevant experience & achievements
- Use JD keywords naturally
- Use only company facts I supply or can verify from an official source.
- End with a confident & strong closing statement
- Follow the posting's format instructions and use readable text.
- Tone: professional, confident, yet engaging, within one page (250-350 words)
```

**Connection to the company**

*"I'm interested/drawn to \[Company\] for its focus on \[specific mission/product/technology\], which aligns with my background in \[your key skill\] and my long-term goal to \[your career goal\]."*  

*"I've followed \[Company\]'s work in \[specific mission/product/technology\] with real interest. It resonates with my experience in \[your key skill\] and my long-term plan to \[your career goal\]."*

 Example

I am particularly interested in **\[XYZ\]** because of your leadership in expanding high-speed connectivity in Nepal and your ongoing investments in network reliability and customer experience, which align directly with my passion for building robust, large-scale networks.

## Introduction Paragraph

Section-wise refinement, Opening

### Opening, Prompt A (Quick)

```text
Write a brief opening for the [position name] role at [company name]. Connect it to [one
relevant skill, project, or result I can verify].
```

### Opening, Prompt B (With Company Value)

```text
Craft an opening paragraph for a cover letter applying for the [position name] role at
[company name]. Highlight my [relevant skill] and express my genuine interest in the
company's [specific company initiative or value].
```

### Opening, Prompt C (Full CV-based, Fresher-Focused)

```text
Act as a professional cover-letter writer for students, freshers, and early-career
applicants. Using my CV and the job description, write the opening paragraph of my cover
letter.

Job title: [PASTE JOB TITLE]  |  Company: [PASTE COMPANY NAME]
My CV: [PASTE CV]  |  JD: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]

- Present me as a serious and capable candidate
- Use my education, projects, skills, certifications, internships, volunteer work, or
  practical learning as evidence
- Do not apologize for lack of experience
- Do not write "Although I do not have experience…"
- Do not invent achievements or skills
- Connect my CV clearly to the job
- Keep it short, strong, and professional, avoid generic or robotic language
- Keep it between 70-100 words
```

## Body Paragraphs (2-3)

Section-wise refinement, Middle

### Body, Prompt A (Step-by-Step Matching)

```text
Act as a recruiter reviewing my CV against this job posting. Write 2-3 concise body
paragraphs that directly address the strongest matches.

Target position: [POSITION NAME]  |  My CV: [Attach or PASTE CV]  |  Job posting: [PASTE JOB AD]

1.  Extract the top 5 job requirements from the job posting
2.  Identify matching evidence from my CV, experience, projects, tools, certifications,
  education, achievements, or leadership
3.  Identify any weak or missing areas honestly
4.  Rules: Do not invent experience  •  Do not overstate weak matches  •  Do not copy the
  job description word for word  •  Use strong but truthful language  •  Focus on employer
  needs
```

### Body, Prompt B (Achievement-based)

```text
Act as a senior recruiter, ATS-aware career coach, and professional cover-letter writer.
Create a customized cover letter body for me. Write a paragraph that highlights how my
qualifications match the needs of a job as a [job title] based on the achievements from my
resume: [paste resume achievements]
```

### Body, Prompt C (Transition / Prior Role)

```text
Write a compelling paragraph for my cover letter that shows how my experience as a [previous
job title] makes me an ideal candidate for the [job title] position at [company name].
Include specific examples of my relevant achievements, from resume: [paste resume]
```

### Body, Prompt D (Skills vs JD Match)

```text
Write a section for my cover letter that explains how my skills and qualifications as listed
in my CV [attached your CV] directly address the main requirements listed in this job
posting for [position name]: [paste job ad]
```

## Closing Paragraph

Section-wise refinement, End strong

### Closing, Prompt A (Full Fresher Version)

```text
Act as a professional cover-letter writer for students and fresh graduates. Write a concise
closing paragraph for my cover letter for [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY NAME].

My strongest strengths are: [PROJECTS / SKILLS / ACADEMIC BACKGROUND / INTERNSHIP / CERTIFICATIONS]

- Sound confident but not overqualified
- Show willingness to learn and contribute
- Mention readiness for an interview or further discussion
- Avoid generic phrases and emotional language
- Keep it between 45-70 words
```

### Closing, Prompt B (Quick Summary)

```text
Write a closing paragraph summarizing my key qualifications for the [job title] role and
expressing my enthusiasm for potentially joining [company name].
```

### Closing, Prompt C (Call to Action)

```text
Write a final paragraph that expresses my excitement about the opportunity to bring my
[specific skills] to [company name] and requests to schedule a time to discuss my
application further.
```
