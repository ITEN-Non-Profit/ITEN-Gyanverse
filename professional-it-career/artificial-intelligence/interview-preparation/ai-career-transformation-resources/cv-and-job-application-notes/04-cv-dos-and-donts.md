# IT CV Dos and Don'ts for ATS and Recruiter Review

IT Education Nepal · © 2026

For Nepali Students & Professionals, Step-by-Step Guide with Real Examples

Students, Bachelor's & Master's in Nepal or abroad

Beginners, Freshers looking for their first IT job

Professionals, Working people want a better CV

Use a readable format, proofread the final file, and tailor relevant evidence to each job description.

The comparison tables show how to write contact details, experience, education, skills, and projects for an IT resume. Dates, employers, and results in the examples are illustrative.

## 1. What is ATS?

An applicant tracking system (ATS) helps employers manage applications. It may parse CVs into fields or support screening rules. Parsing and screening depend on the employer's software and setup.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Use a simple layout and clear section labels to help the parser read your CV. | Rely on complex columns, images, or tables for important CV content. Some parsers miss this information. |
| Use terminology from the job description when it accurately describes your skills. | Add keywords that you cannot support with experience or training. |
| Submit the requested format, such as DOCX or a text-based PDF. Check that you can select and copy the exported text in order. | Submit an image, a scanned PDF without a text layer, or a photo of your CV. |
| Use standard headings: Work Experience, Education, Skills | Use creative headings: 'My Journey', 'Things I Know' |

### Example, Keyword Matching

Job says: **"Python, machine learning, data analysis"**

Avoid: 'I know programming, AI work, and data science'

Do: `Python │ Machine Learning │ Data Analysis`

Use the job description's terminology when it accurately describes what you did.

## 2. CV Format & Length

Keep your CV clean, short, and easy to read. Less is more.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Start with one page if it can show the relevant evidence. | Write 4-5 pages trying to fill space |
| Use a second page when relevant experience needs the space. Follow the employer's instructions. | Use tiny font or small margins to squeeze more in |
| Font: Arial or Calibri, size 10-12 | Mix different fonts or use decorative fonts |
| Use bold only for job titles and company names | Bold every line, it loses meaning |
| Margins: 1 inch (2.5 cm) on all sides | Shrink margins to 0.3 inch to fit more text |

## 3. Contact Information

This goes at the very top of your CV. Keep it professional and short.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Full name, professional email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub (IT roles) | Date of birth, marital status, full home address, photo |
| Professional email: firstname.lastname@example.com | Use a casual username that makes your email hard to recognize. |
| Custom LinkedIn URL: linkedin.com/in/yourname | Default LinkedIn URL with random numbers |
| City and country only: Kathmandu, Nepal | Full street address, ward number, postcode |

### Example, Contact Header

```text
Ram Sharma  │  ram.sharma@example.com  │  [Phone]
[LinkedIn URL]  │  [GitHub URL]  │  Kathmandu, Nepal
```

## 4. Personal Statement

This is 3-4 short sentences at the top of your CV. Tell recruiters who you are, what you can do, and what job you want. Write it **last**, after everything else is done.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| State your degree, key skills, and target job clearly | Start with: 'I am hardworking, passionate and dedicated...' |
| Be specific, name the role and industry you want | Write something so general it could apply to any job |
| Include one strong result or achievement | List hobbies like 'I love music and travelling' |
| Keep it to 3-4 clear sentences | Write a 10-sentence paragraph that no one will read |

### Example, Personal Statement, Formula + Examples

Formula: \[Degree + University\] + \[Key Skills\] + \[One Achievement\] + \[Target Job\]

Do:

**Fresher in Nepal:**  
Final-year BSc CSIT student at TU, Kathmandu. Strong in Python, web development, and databases. Built a leave management system used by 200+ students. Seeking a junior developer role at an IT company in Nepal or abroad.

Do:

**Master's Student Abroad:**  
MSc Data Science student at University of Melbourne. Skilled in Python, machine learning, and SQL. Achieved 92% accuracy in a fraud detection project. Looking for a data analyst internship in Australia.

## 5. Work Experience

This is your most important section. Show what you did and what results you got. Even part-time or freelance work counts.

1.  Write the most recent job first
2.  Start every bullet with a strong action verb: *led, coordinated, organized, mentored, presented, negotiated*
3.  Use action-result bullets: what you did, how, and what changed. Don't write: "Was responsible for web development tasks"
4.  Keep each bullet to one clear sentence

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Developed a student portal used by 500+ users (PHP, MySQL) | 'I helped the team with the website' |
| Reduced page load time by 40% through code optimisation | 'Was responsible for web development tasks' |
| Led 4 developers to deliver the project on time | 'Attended meetings and did coding' |
| Use leadership bullets: led, coordinated, organized, mentored, presented, negotiated | Write generic lines like "participated actively" |
| Include internships, part-time work, and freelance projects | Leave the section blank because 'I have no real experience' |
| Include your prior IT company (if any): Deerwalk, Leapfrog, F1Soft, Yomari | Use vague roles with no company name or date |
| Use present tense for current roles and past tense for completed roles | Mix tense inconsistently |

### Example, Weak vs Strong Bullet Points

Avoid: Worked on the company website

Do: Built an e-commerce site using React and Node.js, increasing online sales by 25%

Avoid: Did data entry tasks

Do: Cleaned 10,000+ records in Excel, reducing data errors by 30%

Avoid: Helped with the mobile app

Do: Developed 3 new features in an Android app (Kotlin) used by 1,200 active users

### Example, Full Work Experience Block

```text
Junior Developer  │  Leapfrog Technology, Kathmandu  │  Jun 2023 to Dec 2023
• Built REST APIs in Node.js for a healthcare app used by 500+ users
• Fixed 20+ bugs in existing code, reducing app crashes by 15%
• Worked in an Agile team of 8 using Jira and GitHub

IT Intern  │  Nepal Telecom, Kathmandu  │  Jan 2023 to May 2023
• Supported network configuration for 10 office branches
• Documented 50+ network diagrams using Cisco Packet Tracer
```

 Quick Tip, No IT Experience Yet?

**Freelance:** 'Built a website for a local restaurant in Pokhara'  
**University:** Write your college projects, team projects  
**Part-time (waiter, shop):** Highlight teamwork, responsibility, and communication

## 6. Education

Keep this short. Most recent degree goes first.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Degree, University, Year, Grade (if strong) | List every subject and module you ever studied |
| Include a grade and its scale when requested or relevant. | Omit a grade when the application requires it. |
| List 4-5 relevant subjects only for your first job | Copy-paste the entire university curriculum |
| List relevant credentials with the official name, issuer, and date. Separate training from earned certification. | Describe a course as an earned certification. |

### Example, Education Block, Nepal Student

```text
BSc Computer Science & Information Technology (CSIT)
Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu  │  2019 to 2023  │  78%
Key Subjects: Data Structures, Web Technology, Database Systems, Networking

Higher Secondary (Science)
Budhanilkantha School, Kathmandu  │  2017 to 2019  │  GPA: 3.7/4.0
```

### Example, Education Block, Abroad Application

```text
MSc Information Technology
University of Queensland, Australia  │  Feb 2023 to Dec 2024  │  GPA: 6.2/7.0

BIT (Bachelor of Information Technology)
Pokhara University, Nepal  │  2018 to 2022  │  82%
```

## 7. Skills Section

Group your skills by category. Be honest. Only list skills you can talk about in an interview.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Group by type: Languages │ Frameworks │ Tools │ Cloud | Write a random list mixing technical and soft skills |
| Be specific: Python (Django, FastAPI), Java (Spring Boot) | Write just 'Python' with no context |
| Be honest about level: Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced | Call yourself 'Expert' in everything, interviewers will test you |

### Example, Skills, IT Student in Nepal

```text
Programming:   Python (Intermediate), PHP (Intermediate), JavaScript (Beginner)
Frameworks:    Laravel, React.js, Django
Database:      MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB
Tools:         Git, GitHub, VS Code, Postman, Figma
Networking:    CCNA-level (Routing, Switching, TCP/IP)
Languages:     Nepali (Native), English (Professional), Hindi (Conversational)
```

## 8. Projects

Projects prove your skills. This section is very important for students and freshers who have little or no job experience.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Name the project and say what problem it solves | Write only 'Final Year Project' with no details |
| List the tools and technologies you used | Leave out what languages or tools you used |
| Show results: users, improvement %, test score | Describe only what the project is, not what you built or achieved |
| Add GitHub or live link if the project is clean | Add a broken or empty GitHub link, or link to a repository without enough information to understand the project. |
| Include: college project, hackathon, freelance, personal build | Skip projects because 'they were just assignments' |

### Example, Project Format + 3 Real Examples

Format: Project Name │ Tech Used │ GitHub link (optional)  
1 sentence: What problem? What did you build? What result?

Do:

**Nepal Fresher:**  
`E-Commerce Site │ PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap │ github.com/ram/shop`  
Built a full online shop for a Kathmandu business with cart and eSewa payment. Now handling 50+ orders per month.

Do:

**University Project:**  
`Student Result System │ Python, Django, PostgreSQL`  
Automated result entry for 300+ students. Cut manual work by 70%.

Do:

**Abroad / Advanced:**  
`Fraud Detection Model │ Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas │ github.com/aadit/fraud`  
Trained an ML model on more than 1 million transactions and deployed it on AWS Lambda. Report the test split, class balance, and precision or recall with any accuracy figure.

## 9. Employment Gaps & Honesty

If you have a gap in your CV, don't hide it. Handle it simply and honestly.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Briefly explain gaps: 'Self-study', 'Family responsibilities' | Leave months or years unexplained, it raises red flags |
| Turn gaps into positives: note courses or projects done | Invent an employer or misstate employment dates. |
| Use accurate dates (month and year) | Round to the year only to hide a short job or gap |
| 'Extended study period, 2021-2023' for exam re-attempts | Pretend gaps don't exist and hope no one notices |

### Example, How to Handle a Gap

**Situation:** 6-month gap after graduation

Avoid: Leave dates blank or skip the period

Do:

**Option 1:**  

```text
Self-Development Period (Jan 2023 to Jun 2023)
• Completed AWS Cloud Practitioner certification
• Built two personal projects (see GitHub portfolio)
```

Do:

**Option 2:**  

```text
Family Responsibility (Jan 2023 to Jun 2023)
• Managed family responsibilities during this period
```

 Watch Out

- Use accurate employer names, dates, and responsibilities.
- State grades as they appear in the official record.
- List certifications you earned and can document.

## 10. Extra Activities & Soft Skills

Don't just say 'I am a good communicator'. Show it. Use a real example or result.

| Do this | Avoid this |
|----|----|
| Club president, event organizer, with what you achieved | 'Member of Computer Club', no achievement shown |
| Quantify: 'Organised tech fest with 400+ students and 12 sponsors' | List hobbies like 'watching movies, listening to music' |
| Hackathon: '4th place, Deerwalk Hackathon 2023, 50 teams' | Skip a 4th place, it is still an achievement |
| Volunteering: 'Taught basic computing to 30 students in Sindhupalchok' | Include anything not relevant like party membership |

### Example, Extra Activities Block

```text
Tech Club President  │  TU Campus, Kathmandu  │  2022-2023
• Organised annual tech event with 400+ students and 10 company sponsors
• Led weekly coding sessions for 80 junior students

Hackathon, 2nd Place  │  Deerwalk Innovate  │  2023
Built a real-time flood alert app for Nepal using IoT sensors and SMS
```

 Quick Tips for Nepal Job Seekers

Nepal IT jobs: **Merojob.com, Jobs.com.np, LinkedIn Nepal, Kaam.com.np**  
Apply early, many companies stop reading applications after a set number  
Follow up once (politely) after 1 week if you hear nothing back  
Do not wait until graduation to build your CV. Build evidence every semester: one project, one leadership activity, one skill proof, and one mentor review.
