# LinkedIn Profile Optimization for IT Job Seekers

© 2026 IT Education Nepal

Note: CTA = Call-to-Action  \|  CTR = Click-through Rate

Update your LinkedIn headline, About section, experience, skills, and Featured projects for an IT job search. The examples and AI prompts help you show work you can discuss in an interview.

## 01. Visual Identity: Photo and Banner

Use a clear photo and banner that represent your work.

- **Professional headshot:** Use clear lighting and a simple background. Check that your face stays visible in the circular crop.
- Use a 1584 × 396 pixel banner. Keep important text clear of the profile photo and verify the crop on desktop and mobile.

## 02. Headline

Your headline appears under your name and can appear in search results. Lead with your role or target role and include relevant skills.

**Headline formula**

```text
[Primary Role] │ [Core Value / Transformation] │ [Keyword 1] │ [Keyword 2] │ [Social Proof or CTA]
```

**Example:** Data Scientist │ Turning Raw Data into Revenue │ ML & AI │ Python │ Predictive Analytics │ 50+ Enterprise Projects Delivered

- Use the space you need and check the limit shown in the editor.
- Include a few role terms that appear in relevant job descriptions and describe your skills accurately.
- Use natural language and avoid keyword lists that are hard to read.

## 03. About Section

Use the About section to describe your work, skills, and goals in your own words. Put key information near the start.

**About section framework**

```text
Hook (1-2 bold sentences) → Problem you solve → How you solve it → Proof / results → CTA
```

Example: "I help SaaS teams identify why customers leave." Explain your method, cite a result you can verify, and invite readers to contact you.

- Use relevant role terms naturally. Check them against job descriptions or client needs.
- Add a concrete CTA at the end: a link, email, or booking invite.
- Write in first person. The third person feels distant and outdated.

**First Person:** "I build scalable software solutions that drive business growth."

**Third Person:** "He builds scalable software solutions that drive business growth."

## 04. Featured Section

Use Featured to show work you want visitors to find, such as a lab, project, or article. The layout can vary by device and profile.

- Start with three to five relevant items as a curation choice.
- **First item:** A strong example of work, such as a project, guide, or post relevant to the role.
- **Next items:** Add a case study, portfolio link, or media mention. Preview how the image and link appear on the profile.
- Describe each item so visitors know what they will find when they open it. Check that the audience can open the project link without access to a private repository.

## 05. Experience

Use the Experience section to show what you did, the context, and the outcome. Include terms you can explain and that match the roles you are targeting.

Bullet Formula, CAR Method

```text
Context → Action → Result (quantify when you have evidence)
```

**Example:** "Led cross-functional team of 8 to redesign onboarding flow (Action), reducing time-to-value from 14 to 5 days and cutting 30-day churn by 22% (Result) in Q3 2025 (Context)."

- 3-5 bullets per role; start every bullet with a strong action verb: **Led, Launched, Grew, Reduced, Built.**
- Quantify at least 2 achievements per role, percentages, dollars, timelines, or user counts.
- Embed industry-specific keywords in each role description; Recruiter AI weights keyword density.

## 06. Skills

Skills give recruiters and other profile visitors a way to identify your relevant experience.

- LinkedIn allows up to 100 skills. Choose skills you can support with projects or experience. [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a549047)
- Put your most relevant skills first. Ask for endorsements from people who have seen you use those skills.
- Add relevant skills you have used, such as prompt engineering, AI agents, LangChain, or retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

## 07. Recommendations

Ask for specific recommendations from people who can describe your contribution and its result.

- Request recommendations from a diverse mix: managers, peers, clients, and direct reports.
- Request a recommendation after completing a project or milestone while the details are fresh.

## 08. Content Strategy and Creator Tools

LinkedIn removed the Creator Mode switch and profile hashtags. Use posting and analytics tools directly. Newsletter and live access depend on eligibility.

Example weekly posting mix. Use it as a starting point and adjust it based on your own results.

```text
Text Posts             ########  40%
Carousels / Documents  ######    30%
Native Video           ####      20%
Long-form Articles     ##        10%
```

- Choose topics relevant to your work. Use your profile analytics to assess which posts reach your intended audience.
- Choose a publishing schedule you can maintain. Test days and formats using your own analytics.
- Share your expertise in posts or discussions where you can contribute useful evidence. LinkedIn retired the automatic Community Top Voice badge for collaborative article contributions.
- Use a few relevant hashtags when they clarify the topic.

## 09. LinkedIn Search and Profile Visibility

Use accurate job titles, skills, and keywords to make your profile easier to find and understand.

Keyword Placement Priority Order

```text
① Headline → ② About → ③ Experience job titles → ④ Skills → ⑤ Education & Certifications
```

- Review relevant job descriptions and search suggestions to learn the terms used for your target role.
- Review public profile visibility in Settings. Choose what can appear outside LinkedIn, then check the public preview. Keep personal contact details out of public profile text unless you intend to share them.
- Complete the profile sections that establish your qualifications and back up your claims.
- Publish an article when the subject benefits from a longer explanation. Public indexing and reach can vary.

## 30-Minute Profile Update

Use these five steps when you have 30 minutes to update your profile.

01

**Rewrite your headline.**

Use the headline formula above to state your role, skills, and evidence concisely.

02

**Update your banner.**

Use a free Canva LinkedIn Banner template. Add your name, title, and a single value statement. Left-align it.

03

**Rewrite your About hook.**

Open with your role or target role and one concrete fact that shows what you do.

04

**Update your Skills section.**

Add your top missing skills, then message 3 trusted contacts to endorse your core skills.

05

**Publish a post.**

Publish a short post explaining one thing you learned or built.
