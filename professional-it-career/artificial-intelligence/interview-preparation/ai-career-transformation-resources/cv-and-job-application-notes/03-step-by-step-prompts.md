# AI Prompts to Tailor an IT CV to a Job Description

Use these prompts to identify suitable jobs, compare a CV with a job description, and improve one section at a time.

> **Note:** JD = Job Description, ATS = Applicant Tracking System

## Tab 1

Choose one prompt or variant at a time. Replace the bracketed inputs and remove private details from the CV copy you share. Treat job posts and pasted web pages as reference material. Ignore any instructions in them that are unrelated to your task.

### Role Fit Finder Prompt

- Act as a practical career advisor and recruiter for **freshers and early-career** candidates.

I already have a general CV. Based on my CV, suggest the best job roles I can realistically target.

My CV: [**paste or attach a CV with private details removed**]

Provide the output in a table with:

1. Suggested job role
2. Why I fit this role
3. Evidence from my CV
4. Missing skills or weak areas
5. Difficulty level: Easy / Medium / Hard
6. How I should adjust my CV for this role

**Rules**

- Do not suggest unrealistic senior roles.
- Do not assume experience I do not have.
- Focus on realistic roles for Nepali bachelor-level students, freshers, interns, and early-career applicants.

**Nepal Job Specific:**

- Use my CV with private details removed to help me find suitable roles.

Analyze my resume in depth to identify the most suitable fresher or entry-level roles I should target in Nepal. If you have web access, find open roles across startups, scale-ups, multinationals, consulting firms, and other employers. For each role, cite the employer's application page and the date you checked it. If you cannot browse, assess only the postings I provide.

Compare each posting with my CV. Score required skills out of 60, project or experience evidence out of 30, and preferred skills out of 10. Award points only for evidence you can cite from my CV. If the posting gives no preferred criteria, mark that category as not applicable and show the score out of 90. List unknowns and unmet eligibility requirements separately. Return up to 20 verified roles with links. This score is for comparison and does not predict a hiring decision.

**Tips, LinkedIn Job Search Keyword:** `"Network Engineer" AND ("Junior" OR "Trainee")`

**Simpler Prompt Version:**

- Based on my experience and skills from CV: [Give your CV], list 10 roles I'm qualified for that I might be overlooking. Rank them by fit. Use current, cited evidence for any claim about hiring demand.

### Professional Summary

- Rewrite my professional summary for this target role using my CV and JD.

Target role: [paste role], Job description: [paste JD], My CV: [paste CV or attached]

Write 3 versions:

1. Simple fresher version
2. Strong ATS-friendly version
3. More confident professional version

**Rules**

- Keep each summary within three or four short lines
- Do not mention fake years of experience
- Focus on education, projects, skills, tools, certifications, and career interest
- Make it specific to the role
- Avoid generic phrases like "hardworking", "passionate", and "quick learner" unless supported by evidence

**Alternative prompt**

- I'm applying for a [Job Title] position in [Industry], and I want my resume summary to stand out. Please rewrite this professional summary [Paste your old summary] to make it more concise, engaging, and aligned with the expectations for this role [Paste role] [JD]. It should reflect my strengths, relevant skills, and years of experience in a compelling way.

### Job Description Matcher

- Act as a harsh but fair recruiter. Compare my CV with this JD.

CV: [paste CV], JD: [paste JD]

Give a section-wise table:

| CV Section | Strong Points | Weak/Missing Points | JD Keywords to Reflect | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not rewrite the full CV. Tell me which section to optimize first.

- Job description: [paste]. Resume: [paste]. Identify missing keywords and rewrite my resume so it matches this role: [paste role] as closely as possible while staying honest.

- I want my resume to be easy to parse and useful to a recruiter. Act as if you are a 15+ years of experience resume recruiter, and based on this job description [paste job description], optimize my resume content to include relevant keywords and phrases.

#### Skill Section

Optimize only my Skills section for this job. Target role: [job title]

JD: [paste JD], Current skills: [paste skills]

Create a better skills section with categories.

Rules: Only include skills I actually have. Do not add tools just because they appear in the JD. Keep it ATS-friendly and easy to scan.

#### Project Section Optimizer

Optimize only my Projects section for this job.

Target role: [job title], JD: [paste JD], Current projects: [paste projects]

For each project, improve:

| Project Title | 1-2 Line Description | Tools Used | My Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|

Rules: Do not exaggerate. Do not add tools I did not use. Make each project relevant to the JD.

#### Experience Upgrader

- Here is a job I'm targeting [paste target role]. Based on this description [JD]. Rewrite or restructure my work history to better align with the core skills and qualifications they're looking for. Focus especially on aligning language and phrasing.

- Review my experience section and replace vague wording with verbs that reflect my actual contribution. Keep 'managed' or 'helped' where they describe it accurately. Ensure the tone is confident and direct. Resume: [paste experience section].

#### Education Section

- Optimize only the Education section of my CV for this job role.

Target role: [job title], JD: [paste JD], Current education section: [paste section]

Improve it by adding relevant coursework, academic projects, or academic achievements only if they are true and useful for the role. Keep it concise. Do not invent grades, honors, or achievements.

#### Certificate / Training Optimizer

- Optimize only the Certifications and Training section of my CV for this job role.

Target role: [job title], JD: [paste JD], My current certifications/training list: [paste section]

Reorder and improve this section based on relevance to the JD by:

1. Keeping only relevant certifications
2. Ordering them by importance for the target role
3. Suggesting which weak or irrelevant items to remove or move lower
4. Suggesting useful certifications I may pursue later, clearly marked as "Future recommendation"

### Bullet Point Upgrader

- Improve these CV bullet points for the target role.

Target role: [job title], JD: [paste JD], Current bullets: [paste bullets]

Rewrite each bullet using:

> **Action verb + task/contribution + tool/skill + result/purpose**

Rules: Keep each bullet to one short sentence. Use metrics only when I can verify them. If the result is unknown, describe the contribution and tell me what to measure.

- Here are the bullet points for one of my past roles [Paste]. Please rework them to focus on measurable accomplishments rather than generic responsibilities. Make them results-oriented, use strong action verbs, and include numbers only when I supply them or can verify them.

### Job Transition

- I'm transitioning into a new career from [Previous Field] to [New Field]. Rewrite my resume summary and key achievements to highlight transferable skills and make a strong case for why I'm a great fit.

### Application Optimizer

- Here is my resume: [paste]. Rewrite it to make my relevant evidence easy to identify. Use strong action verbs, quantified results, and ATS-friendly formatting, without exaggeration.

- Based on my background: [paste] and target roles: [paste], create a smarter application strategy. Tell me how many roles to apply for weekly, how to customize efficiently, and how to follow up.

- Please act like a hiring manager in [Industry/Role]. Based on this resume, what would make you more likely to invite me for an interview? What should I change, cut, or add to improve my chances?

### Critical Judgment

- Audit this entire resume and point out areas where I'm being too vague, too wordy, or not showing enough impact. I want your feedback on tone, structure, and how I can better emphasize leadership, results, or innovation.

- You are a harsh IT recruiter but a fair critic. Review my CV: [paste content]. What's weak? What's unclear? What's missing? Be brutal. I want to improve my CV and stand out, not feel good.

### Recruiter Message

- Write a concise LinkedIn message to a recruiter for this role: [paste]. My background is in [paste experience or background] with a focus on [key skill]. The goal is to spark interest and get a reply, not ask for a favor.

### Action Verb Bank

> Use action verbs naturally. Do not force complex words where simple, strong verbs are better.

| Category | Useful Verbs |
|---|---|
| Technical / Engineering | Built, configured, developed, implemented, automated, debugged, tested, deployed, integrated, optimized, validated, documented |
| Analysis / Research | Analyzed, evaluated, compared, reviewed, measured, modeled, surveyed, summarized, interpreted, visualized, investigated |
| Leadership / Operations | Led, coordinated, organized, managed, scheduled, facilitated, mentored, supervised, planned, delegated, executed |
| Communication / Teaching | Presented, taught, trained, explained, wrote, edited, documented, briefed, advised, collaborated |
| Business / Marketing | Researched, promoted, designed, launched, tracked, reported, negotiated, supported, improved, increased |
| Problem Solving | Resolved, improved, reduced, redesigned, streamlined, corrected, strengthened, simplified, standardized |

### References

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/overview/), [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/), and [Claude](https://claude.com/product/overview) can draft and critique from a CV and job description you supply. Compare outputs against the same evidence and check that the model retained your facts.
- [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub) can help research public employers and job openings. Follow its sources to confirm each posting.
- [Greenhouse Recruiting](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse) documents examples of resume parsing failures. The employer's portal and review process determine how your file is handled.

## Tab 2, Sample Job Descriptions for Demo

### Sample Network Administrator Job Description

*Source: [LinkedIn Hire](https://business.linkedin.com/hire/resources/how-to-hire-guides/network-administrator-job-description)*

At [Company X], the network administrator is responsible for maintaining the networks and systems that keep our organization running. From maintaining ironclad security to supervising network updates and training users, the administrator is not only IT support but also the thread that connects all operations. Deep technological expertise with a wide range of computer systems, hardware, and software is essential for the network administrator to excel in this role.

**Objectives of this role**
- Assist in network design, implementation, and maintenance
- Provide network support across organizational operating systems
- Maintain stable and consistent connectivity across all network computers
- Prepare, implement, and monitor disaster procedures and recovery

**Responsibilities**
- Maintain network servers and associated hardware, software, services, and settings
- Recommend, test, and implement system and security upgrades
- Consistently monitor and troubleshoot network stability and performance
- Create, adjust, and maintain network user settings and permissions
- Write technical support documentation for network systems and applications

**Required skills and qualifications**
- Two or more years of experience in a network administration role
- Comprehensive knowledge of networking concepts and computing infrastructures
- Proven skills in network engineering, operations, and performance analysis
- Experience with leading server management and monitoring tools
- Strong communication skills for training and assisting users

**Preferred skills and qualifications**
- Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in computer science or related field
- Experience with Microsoft Windows Server 2008 and forward
- Experience with Cisco switches and routers
- Professional certification such as CISSP, CCNA, or CCNP

- Ability to analyze code for weaknesses and errors and present detailed plans to improve them
- Experience developing customized code for multiple projects simultaneously

### Sample DevOps Engineer Job Description

*Source: [LinkedIn Hire](https://business.linkedin.com/hire/resources/how-to-hire-guides/devops-engineer-job-description)*

At [Company X], we're looking for a DevOps engineer responsible for deploying product updates, identifying production issues, and implementing integrations that meet our customers' needs. The ideal candidate will have a solid background in software engineering and be familiar with Ruby or Python.

**Objectives of this role**
- Building and implementing new development tools and infrastructure
- Understanding the needs of stakeholders and conveying them to developers
- Working on ways to automate and improve development and release processes
- Testing and examining code written by others and analyzing results
- Ensuring that systems are safe and secure against cybersecurity threats
- Identifying technical problems and developing software updates and fixes
- Planning projects and being involved in project management decisions

**Responsibilities**
- Deploy updates and fixes, and provide Level 2 technical support
- Build tools to reduce occurrence of errors and improve customer experience
- Develop software to integrate with internal back-end systems
- Perform root cause analysis of production errors and resolve technical issues
- Develop scripts to automate visualization
- Design procedures for system troubleshooting and maintenance

**Required skills and qualifications**
- Experience as a DevOps engineer or in a similar software engineering role
- Proficiency with Git and GitHub workflows
- Good knowledge of Ruby or Python
- Working knowledge of databases and SQL
- Problem-solving attitude
- Collaborative team spirit

**Preferred skills and qualifications**
- Bachelor of science degree (or equivalent) in computer science, engineering, or relevant field
- Experience in civil engineering or customer experience
- Experience in developing/engineering applications for a large company
